🤔 What Is This?

Do you have an old iMac (from around 2010) sitting in a corner gathering dust? Instead of letting it waste away, turn it into a beautiful second display for your newer Apple Silicon Mac (like the M1, M2, or M3 MacBooks or Mac minis).

This software streams your Mac's screen to your old iMac wirelessly with incredibly low lag. It uses your hardware's built-in H.264 encoder to send video over your network at lightning speed. You get a smooth, responsive second screen that feels almost like a wired connection.

The best part? It's completely free and easy to set up — no technical knowledge required!

🔧 How It Works (Simple Version)

  1. Install on your iMac: You download a small program onto your old iMac (running OS X 10.9). This program receives the video and shows it on screen.
  2. Install on your Apple Silicon Mac: You run a small utility on your newer Mac that sends its screen to the iMac.
  3. Connect: Both computers need to be on the same network. That's it — your iMac instantly becomes a second screen!
💡 Tip: For the best experience, connect both computers to the same Wi-Fi router, or better yet, use an Ethernet cable for your iMac if possible. This ensures the fastest data transfer.

🛠️ Troubleshooting Tips

Sometimes things don't work perfectly right away. Here are some common fixes:

  • Screen is laggy: Try moving your iMac closer to your Wi-Fi router, or connect both computers with Ethernet cables if possible. Close any heavy apps running on the iMac.
  • Nothing shows up: Double-check that both computers are on the exact same network. If you have a guest network or a VPN, turn those off temporarily.
  • App won't start: Make sure your iMac's operating system is version 10.9 or newer. If it's older, you may need to update it first.
  • Picture quality is low: This usually means your network is slow. Try a wired connection for the iMac to get the best quality.

If you still have issues, check the Issues section on the GitHub page — other users may have found solutions to the same problem.
