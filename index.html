About me

Hi, I'm Benlu. I am a PhD student at UMass Amherst in the BioNLP group.

My research focuses on medical reasoning and evaluation in language models, including multimodal question answering and evidence-based medical calculations. I study how models use clinical and visual evidence, where their reasoning fails, and how to evaluate and improve it.

My work spans benchmarks for medical visual reasoning, step-by-step evaluation of clinical calculations, and language models for medical procedure coding.
